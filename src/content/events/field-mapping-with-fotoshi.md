Join us on **Monday, November 30th** around Politecnico di Milano (Campus Leonardo) for a collaborative afternoon of field mapping.
We'll survey shops, amenities, and points of interest (POIs) across Città Studi to improve OpenStreetMap (OSM) and test new mapping tools.

We will be joined by Chris Beddow, CTO at [Fotoshi](https://fotoshi.com/), who will kick off the event by introducing this newly launched platform for capturing photos of places around the world and contributing them directly to OSM.

Next we will split into small groups and head out into the neighborhood to survey local businesses and POIs.
You can experiment with the Fotoshi app or use any OSM field editor you prefer.

Finally, we'll return to the classroom to review our work and test the Fotoshi editor to turn the photos taken during the survey into live OpenStreetMap updates. Bring your laptop and a mouse if you'd like to help with desktop editing!

**The event is open to everyone**, whether you are a Politecnico student or not. No prior mapping experience or registration required.

**The introductory talk will be held in English**

### How to prepare

Before the event, we recommend that you:

- [create an OpenStreetMap account](https://www.openstreetmap.org/user/new) if you don't already have one;
- install [Fotoshi](https://fotoshi.com/), create an account, and log in to capture POI photos with us;
- install [StreetComplete](https://streetcomplete.app/) (now also available on iOS!) or [Every Door](https://every-door.app/) (Android and iOS) and log in if you want a more traditional field mapping experience.

Remember to fully charge your phone and bring a power bank if you have one!
